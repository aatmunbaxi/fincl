#+title: Interest-rate instruments

Use the package/readtable setup in [[./getting-started.org]] first. These
examples use flat curves to keep the construction short. Dated or
bootstrapped curves can replace them; see [[./markets-and-curves.org]].

* Curves and a fixed-rate bond
A rates-only market needs no equity underlying. Discounting maps currency
to curve; an index names its forecast curve separately.
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *rates-market*
  (make-market :valuation-date #D"2026-10-01"
               :curves '((:eur-ois :rate 0.025d0) (:euribor-6m :rate 0.03d0))
               :discounting '((:eur . :eur-ois))))

(defparameter *bond-schedule*
  (make-schedule #D"2026-10-01" #D"2031-10-01" #T"1Y" :calendar +target+))

(defparameter *bond*
  (make-fixed-rate-bond *bond-schedule* 0.035d0 +actual-365-fixed+
                        :face 1000 :currency :eur :settlement-days 2))

(price *bond* :discounting *rates-market*)
;; => 1044.9754377424476d0
;;    NIL
;;    #<ENGINE (make-engine :discounting)>

(bond-clean-price *bond* *rates-market*)
;; => 104.48782099611151d0

(bond-dirty-price *bond* *rates-market*)
;; => 104.52617716049508d0
#+end_src
PRICE gives the currency NPV at valuation. Clean/dirty prices are per 100
of face at settlement; dirty includes accrued interest. BOND-YIELD,
BOND-DURATION, BOND-CONVEXITY, and Z-SPREAD use explicit quote conventions.

* A payer swap
The fixed payer receives floating. Schedules, day counts, and index conventions
are explicit; the market supplies forecasts and discount factors.
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *fixed-schedule*
  (make-schedule #D"2027-10-01" #D"2032-10-01" #T"1Y" :calendar +target+))

(defparameter *float-schedule*
  (make-schedule #D"2027-10-01" #D"2032-10-01" #T"6M" :calendar +target+))

(defparameter *swap*
  (make-vanilla-swap :payer 1000000
                     *fixed-schedule* 0.03d0
                     (make-instance 'thirty-360 :variant :bond-basis)
                     *float-schedule* (euribor #T"6M" :forecast-curve :euribor-6m)))

(price *swap* :discounting *rates-market*)
;; => 2037.1977964745602d0
;;    NIL
;;    #<ENGINE (make-engine :discounting)>

(fair-rate *swap* *rates-market*)
;; => 0.0304500457396286d0 (3.04500457396286d0%)
#+end_src
OIS uses MAKE-OIS-SWAP with an overnight index such as SOFR or ESTR. FRAs
use MAKE-FORWARD-RATE-AGREEMENT. Both have discounting-engine routes.

Past index observations belong in MAKE-MARKET's =:fixings= argument, for
example =((:euribor-6m ("2026-09-29" 0.03d0)))=. Before valuation, an absent
required fixing signals MISSING-FIXING. On the valuation date, an available
fixing is used; otherwise the index forecasts. The swap here starts in the
future, so no historical fixings are required.

* European swaption: quoted vol
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *swaption*
  (make-swaption *swap*
                 (make-instance 'european-exercise :expiry #D"2027-09-29")
                 :settlement :physical))

(price *swaption* (make-engine :black-rates :volatility 0.20d0) *rates-market*)

(price *swaption* (make-engine :bachelier-rates :volatility 0.008d0) *rates-market*)
#+end_src
These are different quotes: 20% lognormal versus 80 basis points normal
vol per square-root year. They are not meant to give the same price.
For shifted Black, supply =:displacement=. A RATES-VOL-STRUCTURE can replace
the number; EXPIRY-RATES-VOL interpolates total variance between expiries.
Caps/floors/collars use these same engines with optionlet volatility. Here
is a cap on the swap's floating leg (not a swaption volatility quote):
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *cap*
  (make-cap-floor :cap (second (swap-legs *swap*)) :cap-rate 0.04d0))
(price *cap* (make-engine :bachelier-rates :volatility 0.008d0) *rates-market*)
#+end_src

* Swaption: a short-rate model
Hull-White engines use one model curve for forecasting and discounting.
Use a single-curve example when comparing its formulas or tree.
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *single-curve-market*
  (make-market :valuation-date #D"2026-10-01"
               :curves '((:euribor-6m :rate 0.03d0))
               :discounting '((:eur . :euribor-6m))))

(defparameter *hw* (make-hull-white :a 0.05d0 :sigma 0.01d0))

(price *swaption* (make-engine :jamshidian :process *hw*) *single-curve-market*)
;; => 16867.247938661803d0
;;    NIL
;;    #<ENGINE (make-engine :jamshidian)>

(price *swaption*
       (make-engine :hull-white-tree :process *hw* :time-steps 100)
       ,*single-curve-market*)
;; => 16867.58390145408d0
;;    NIL
;;    #<ENGINE (make-engine :hull-white-tree :time-steps 100)>
#+end_src
Jamshidian supports European physical settlement without floating spread.
The tree also handles physical Bermudan swaptions and callable fixed-rate
bonds, putting exercise/call dates on its grid. Hull-White sigma is a model
parameter; it is not the Black swaption volatility from the previous example.
Unlike equity options, rates instrument pricing does not accept =:auto= selection.
