#+title: What goes into a price?

* Pricing Instruments and Contracts
A valuation combines contract terms, market data, and a pricing method:
#+begin_src lisp
(price instrument engine market)
#+end_src
An option also needs assumptions about the underlying's dynamics. Some
engines fix those assumptions: Black-Scholes requires GBM. Others take a
process, such as Heston, separately from their numerical settings.

| Ingredient | Data Contained                    | Example                           |
|------------+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------|
| Instrument | Terms agreed in the contract      | Strike, expiry, coupon schedule   |
| Market     | Data observed on a valuation date | Spot, curves, fixings, quoted vol |
| Process    | Assumed dynamics                  | GBM, Heston, Hull-White           |
| Engine     | Formula or numerical method       | Black formula, tree, Monte Carlo  |

A fixed-rate bond needs cash flows and discount factors.
A swap also needs a forecast curve and past fixings.
A rate option adds either quoted rate volatility or a short-rate model.

=price= returns three values: NPV, sampling standard error, and the engine used.
The second value is =nil= for deterministic methods, but that doesn't mean zero
approximation error. Bond NPV is a currency amount; clean/dirty bond prices are
per 100 of face.

* Instruments and methods
This is a summary of existing routes, not every possible parameter regime.

| Instrument               | Pricing route                                      | Caveat                                                          |
|--------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| European vanilla option  | Black-Scholes, COS, MC; Merton/CEV formulas        | Formula must match the process                                  |
| American vanilla option  | GBM binomial, BAW, COS, MC with LSM                | BAW has domain limits; LSM approximates exercise                |
| Bermudan vanilla option  | GBM binomial, COS, MC with LSM                     | Equity tree/LSM snap dates to their grids                       |
| Custom terminal payoff   | Monte Carlo; LSM for early exercise                | No automatic custom analytic formula                            |
| Barrier option           | Reiner-Rubinstein (+BGK for dates); MC with bridge | European; continuous barrier needs a diffusion for the bridge   |
| Asian option             | Geometric, fixed strike: closed form; MC otherwise | European; observation dates must follow the valuation date      |
| Lookback option          | Monte Carlo                                        | European; vanilla call/put kind only                            |
| Structured/path payoff   | Monte Carlo through =define-path-payoff=             | European; no closed form; see [[./exotics-and-monte-carlo.org]]     |
| Fixed-rate bond          | Discounting                                        | Clean/dirty price, yield, duration, convexity, z-spread helpers |
| Vanilla Ibor swap or OIS | Discounting                                        | Forecast curve, discount curve, and needed fixings              |
| FRA                      | Discounting                                        | Index forecast/fixing and settlement conventions                |
| Cap, floor, collar       | Black or Bachelier rates engine                    | Vol is an optionlet quote, not equity vol                       |
| European swaption        | Black/Bachelier rates; Hull-White Jamshidian/tree  | Settlement and model restrictions below                         |
| Bermudan swaption        | Hull-White tree                                    | Physical settlement                                             |
| Callable fixed-rate bond | Hull-White tree                                    | Explicit call/put schedule                                      |
Key:
- MC :: Monte Carlo
- COS :: Fourier-cosine method of Fang and Osterlee
- LSM :: Longstaff-Schwarz
- BAW :: Approximation by Barone and Adesi-Whaley
- GBM :: Geometric Brownian motion
Black/Bachelier swaptions accept physical or cash-annuity settlement.
Jamshidian requires European physical settlement and no floating spread.
Hull-White uses one model curve for forecasting and discounting; it is not
a general stochastic multi-curve model.

Equity cash-dividend schedules and an equity PDE engine are not implemented
yet, but are planned. Seasoned path-dependent options (fixings before the
valuation date) are not supported. Smiles, local vol and model calibration
are in [[./surfaces-and-calibration.org]].

* Equity processes
COS is the Fourier-cosine method of Fang and Osterlee. It needs a characteristic function for
Europeans, and independent increments for early exercise.

| Process                   | European COS             | Monte Carlo             | Early-exercise COS |
|---------------------------+--------------------------+-------------------------+--------------------|
| GBM                       | Yes                      | Yes                     | Calls and puts     |
| Heston                    | Yes                      | Yes                     | No                 |
| Bates (Heston with jumps) | Yes                      | Yes                     | No                 |
| Merton jump diffusion     | Yes; also series formula | Yes                     | Puts               |
| Kou jump diffusion        | Yes                      | Yes                     | Puts               |
| Variance gamma            | Yes                      | Yes                     | Puts               |
| NIG                       | Yes                      | Yes                     | Puts               |
| CGMY                      | Yes                      | No sampler              | Puts               |
| CEV                       | No; separate CEV formula | Yes                     | No                 |
| Dupire local vol          | No                       | Yes (needs a surface)   | No                 |
| GARCH                     | No                       | Yes, model-period steps | No                 |

The CEV formula supports beta <= 1 within its numerical domain. COS early
exercise for non-GBM processes currently supports puts; its call symmetry
route is GBM-specific. MC early exercise is available through LSM for the
simulatable processes, subject to their stepping requirements.

* Curves, rates, and volatilities
The market stores named yield curves and a currency-to-discount-curve map.
An equity underlying has a spot, currency, dividend curve, and Black-vol
structure. Pricing narrows a composite market to that underlying's view.
Rates instruments use the composite market's named curves directly; no
placeholder equity underlying is needed.

- A numeric curve rate is continuously compounded. Dated curves can store
  discount factors, zero rates, or forwards. Bootstrapping builds a curve
  from deposit, FRA, futures, swap, or OIS helpers.
- An INTEREST-RATE value carries its day count, compounding, and frequency.
  That is different from an unqualified numeric curve rate.
- A market has a day count for converting dates to pricing time. Dated
  curves have their own reference dates and day counts; adapters reconcile them.
- Equity flat vol is a Black quote. Default GBM reads the market's ATM-forward
  variance term structure; this does not make GBM reproduce an arbitrary smile.
- Rate-option vols are supplied to their engine, flat or by expiry. Black
  uses relative lognormal vol; Bachelier uses absolute normal rate vol.
- Hull-White takes mean reversion and short-rate volatility, and fits the
  initial curve. These are model parameters, not swaption quote volatilities.

* Choosing a route
For equity options, =:auto= assumes GBM using market vol. Supplying a process in
place of an engine also requests automatic engine selection under that process.
No better engine choice falls back to Monte Carlo with a warning; selection is
not a promise of a given numerical tolerance. Rates instruments require an
explicit engine such as =:discounting=.
