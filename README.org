* Option Pricing

As of now, a few option pricers are available, classed broadly under numerical and analytic/analytic approximation.

Vanilla European options have analytic prices under the assumption that the spot price follows a geometric Brownian motion. A Monte Carlo pricer is also available. Vanilla American option values are approximated analytically using the method of Barone-Adesi and Whaley under the same assumptions.
