#+title: Vol surfaces, local vol and calibration

Setup as in [[./grand-tour.org]]; this page uses its own market and a
synthetic "market" generated by a known Heston model, so you can see the
calibration recover it.
#+begin_src lisp
(ql:quickload :fincl)
(defpackage #:surfaces (:use #:cl #:fincl))
(in-package #:surfaces)
(named-readtables:in-readtable fincl:syntax)
(defparameter +today+ #D"2026-10-01")
(defparameter *m*
  (make-bs-market :valuation-date +today+ :spot 100 :rate 0.04d0
                  :dividend 0.01d0 :vol 0.22d0))
(defun months-out (n) (add-tenor +today+ (make-tenor n :months)))
(defun otm (k expiry)
  (if (< k 100) (make-option (put k) (expires expiry)) (make-option (call k) (expires expiry))))

(defparameter *truth* (make-heston :v0 0.04d0 :theta 0.05d0 :kappa 1.5d0 :xi 0.6d0 :rho -0.7d0))
(defparameter *quotes*
  (quote-set
   (mapcan (lambda (months)
             (mapcar (lambda (k)
                       (make-option-quote
                        (otm k (months-out months))
                        :price (price (otm k (months-out months))
                                      (make-engine :cos :process *truth*) *m*)))
                     '(70 80 90 100 110 120 130)))
           '(1 3 6 12 24))))
#+end_src

* Quotes
=make-option-quote= takes a European vanilla and either a =:price= or a Black
implied =:vol=, plus a =:weight= for fits. =quote-implied-vol= and
=quote-price= convert between them on a market (forward, discount and expiry
come from it).
#+begin_src lisp
(let ((q (first (quote-set-quotes *quotes*))))
  (list (quote-price q *m*) (quote-implied-vol q *m*)))
#+end_src

* Surfaces
A surface is total variance /w(k, T)/ in log-forward moneyness /k = ln(K/F)/.
It answers the ordinary vol protocol through the forward, so every engine
that reads =black-variance= (Black–Scholes, BAW, the tree) uses it, and
=bump= moves every implied vol.
| Constructor                         | What                                                              |
|-------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =quotes-vol-surface=                | cubic smile per quoted expiry, /w/ linear in /T/ between          |
| =make-grid-vol-surface=             | the same from expiries × log-moneyness × vols                     |
| =make-svi-slice=, =make-svi-surface= | raw SVI per expiry                                                |
| =make-ssvi-surface=                 | Gatheral–Jacquier SSVI with power-law /phi/                       |
| =make-sabr-smile=                   | Hagan 2002 lognormal, with Obłój's correction (default)           |
Calendar arbitrage (/w/ falling in /T/) signals =calendar-arbitrage= at
construction; =butterfly-arbitrage-free-p= and =calendar-arbitrage-free-p=
check Gatheral's /g(k) >= 0/ and monotonicity in /T/ on dense grids.
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *grid* (quotes-vol-surface *quotes* *m*))
(defparameter *smiled*
  (make-bs-market :valuation-date +today+ :spot 100 :rate 0.04d0 :dividend 0.01d0 :vol *grid*))
(list (price (otm 90 (months-out 12)) :black-scholes *smiled*)
      (price (otm 90 (months-out 12)) (make-engine :cos :process *truth*) *m*)) ; same, up to interpolation
#+end_src
SSVI has an arbitrage-free parameter region, enforced at construction:
=eta (1 + |rho|) <= 2= and =0 < gamma <= 1/2=.

* Fitting smiles
Each fitter takes quotes and the market and returns =(values fit vol-rmse)=:
=fit-svi= (one expiry; Zeletskii–De Marco quasi-explicit reduction, then a
Levenberg–Marquardt polish), =fit-ssvi= (several expiries), =fit-sabr= (one
expiry; =:beta= fixes β).
#+begin_src lisp
(multiple-value-bind (surface rmse) (fit-ssvi *quotes* *m*) rmse)
(let* ((one-year (quote-set (remove-if-not
                             (lambda (q) (date= (expiry (option-exercise (quote-instrument q)))
                                                (months-out 12)))
                             (quote-set-quotes *quotes*)))))
  (multiple-value-bind (slice rmse) (fit-svi one-year *m*)
    (list slice rmse (butterfly-arbitrage-free-p slice))))
#+end_src
Hagan's SABR formulas (lognormal and normal) match QuantLib's
=sabrVolatility= to 1e-8 on 66 cases (=tests/fixtures/sabr.sexp=).

* Dupire local volatility
=(make-local-vol)= reads the market's surface (or =:surface=) and builds
σ_loc from Gatheral's total-variance form with analytic derivatives. Prices
of Europeans reproduce the surface by construction; exotics then differ from
both Black–Scholes and a calibrated stochastic-vol model, which is the point:
#+begin_src lisp
(defparameter *dko* (make-option (call 100) (barrier :down-and-out 80)
                                 (expires #T"1Y" :from +today+)))
(with-pricing-kernel ()
  (list (price *dko* (make-engine :mc :process (make-local-vol) :n-paths 50000 :n-steps 100) *smiled*)
        (price *dko* (make-engine :mc :process *truth* :n-paths 50000 :n-steps 100) *m*)
        (price *dko* :black-scholes *m*)))
#+end_src
A non-positive Dupire denominator (butterfly arbitrage in the surface)
signals =butterfly-arbitrage=. =:substeps= (default 4) sets the Euler steps
inside each grid step; with 1 and a strike-flat surface the paths are GBM's
exactly.

* Calibrating any process
=(calibrate process market quotes &key engine objective method fixed)=
returns =(values calibrated-process report)=. Parameters, their order and
bounds come from the process class (=process-parameters=,
=parameter-bounds=); the objective reprices every quote with =engine= and
compares Black implied vols (=:implied-vol-rmse=, default) or prices
(=:price-rmse=), each scaled by the quote's weight.
#+begin_src lisp
(multiple-value-bind (fitted report)
    (calibrate (make-heston :v0 0.03d0 :theta 0.03d0 :kappa 3d0 :xi 0.2d0 :rho 0d0)
               *m* *quotes* :engine :cos)
  (list (getf report :rmse) (getf report :parameters)))

;; Hold kappa fixed (by name, or (name value)):
(multiple-value-bind (fitted report) (calibrate (make-heston :kappa 2d0) *m* *quotes*
                                                :engine :cos :fixed '(:kappa))
  (getf report :parameters))
#+end_src
The report is a plist: =:rmse :max-error :iterations :residuals
:parameters=. Quotes are repriced in parallel under the bound kernel (serially
for Monte Carlo engines) and the result does not depend on the worker count.
Methods: =:levenberg-marquardt= (default; central-difference Jacobian),
=:nelder-mead=, =:differential-evolution=. Heston is nearly degenerate as
κ → 0, so prefer Levenberg–Marquardt or bound κ away from zero. With a Monte
Carlo engine use a fixed seed (the default counter RNG gives common random
numbers across trial parameters, so the objective is smooth).

A calibrated process is an ordinary process object: pass it to =price=, to
=with-engines=, or to =make-engine=.
